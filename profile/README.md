## Who we are

GDG on Campus EPITA is the EPITA chapter of [Google Developer Groups on Campus](https://developers.google.com/community/gdg). We learn and grow together by building practical projects with Google technologies and methodology. Everyone is welcome, whatever your technical background or year of study.

## What we do

| | |
|---|---|
| 🛠️ **Workshops** | Hands-on sessions to get started with a technology in an evening |
| 🎤 **Talks** | Students and engineers share their internships, their tools and their work |
| 🏆 **Hackathons** | A weekend to build something from scratch, in teams |
| 💻 **Projects** | Real code, reviewed like in a company: one task, one branch, one pull request |
